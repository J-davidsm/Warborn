# Acadania

## Short description
Command a fantasy kingdom in turn-based hex battles. Capture settlements, specialize your army, and outmaneuver rival kingdoms.

## Full description
Lead knights, archers, assassins, siege engines, and dragons across painted battlefields. Every move matters: terrain changes movement and defense, settlements fund your army, and controlled territory determines how large a force you can support.

Choose military doctrines that suit your strategy. Protect your support units, use diplomacy to negotiate with rival kingdoms, and build fortifications to secure your borders.

Start with Captain Garran's guided tutorial, play campaigns and skirmishes, survive Endless mode, or create and save your own scenarios in the map editor. Online rooms support two to four players and shared battle-generation settings; cooperative Endless is also available.

Browser beta for desktop mouse and keyboard. Saves are stored in your browser and do not automatically transfer between websites or devices. Online play requires a working connection to the game's public multiplayer services.

## Controls
- Click a friendly unit to select it, then click a highlighted destination to move.
- Select an attacker and click a valid enemy target to attack.
- Click terrain or a unit to inspect its information.
- Click a settlement to recruit, upgrade, or open the doctrine tree.
- Drag the map to pan; use the mouse wheel to zoom.
- Choose End Turn when you have finished your actions.
- Use Save Scenario to create a checkpoint; resume through Commands → Saved battles → Load Level.
- Escape closes the recruitment menu. The music button controls both music and effects.
